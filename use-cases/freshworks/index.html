Employee journeys and service alerts

Conductor supports configurable employee journeys from onboarding to offboarding. The shared workflow platform also coordinates alert-management processes.

Enterprise application integration

Freshworks’ Conductor-based integration platform coordinates long-running workflows across enterprise systems, using reusable connectors and event-driven updates to manage service handoffs.

Enterprise content synchronization

Conductor coordinates content connectors for Google Drive, Confluence and SharePoint, combining scheduling, retries and recovery in the synchronization workflows.

Account deletion

Conductor orchestrates account-deletion workflows, coordinating the dependent services involved in carrying out account removal.

Process Automation

Workflow completion notifications

Freshworks contributed a Conductor change that prevents repeated termination of the same workflow. The implementation avoids duplicate lifecycle notifications and repeated work against supporting queues and databases.

Conversational analytics workflows

Freshworks used Conductor for conversational-analytics workflows and tuned workflow polling as part of platform operations. That application subsequently moved toward an agentic implementation.
