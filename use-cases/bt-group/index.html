Orders and Fulfillment

Broadband checkout and fulfillment

Conductor coordinates broadband checkout and fulfillment workflows, including Business in a Box services. The workflows connect customer orders with the services and customer-care tools that carry them forward.

Orders and Fulfillment

Returns across courier networks

Conductor connects return-processing services with multiple courier systems. Kafka events trigger and connect the asynchronous work required to manage different return scenarios.

Process Automation

Subscription and insurance commerce

Conductor coordinates distributed services within a commerce platform for subscriptions and insurance. Shared workflow execution gives engineering teams visibility into progress and failures across participating services.

Orders and Fulfillment

Marketplace seller and order workflows

Conductor coordinates seller onboarding, product, order and fulfillment services in BT's business marketplace environment. Conductor also forms part of the integration architecture for EE Consumer Marketplace.

Process Automation

Event-driven microservice workflows

BT uses Conductor with Kafka to coordinate complex, event-driven workflows across microservices. The orchestration supports a distributed architecture developed as part of the company’s platform modernization.
