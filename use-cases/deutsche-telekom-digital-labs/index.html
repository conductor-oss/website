Data Integration

Product and tariff updates

Conductor supports OneShop’s commerce platform, including workflows that propagate tariff and product changes from a single administrative update across the application.

Orders and Fulfillment

Device trade in

Conductor coordinates services in OneShop’s device trade-in application, connecting the processing used to manage the trade-in journey.

Data Lifecycle Management

Shopping cart data retention

OneShop uses scheduled Conductor workflows to remove inactive shopping-cart data in off-peak batches. The workflows coordinate country-specific tasks and dependent database-table cleanup.
