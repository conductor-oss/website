Data Pipelines and Analytics

Fab incident investigation

Conductor selects, retrieves and transforms plant data when systems at a semiconductor fab encounter an issue. The workflow prepares the relevant information for IT operations teams and plant users, helping them investigate the incident with the required data in one place.

Infrastructure Lifecycle

Failover readiness checks

Conductor coordinates server-failover pre-checks before a transition from a primary server to its backup. The workflow organizes the readiness tasks that help teams assess whether the change can proceed correctly.

Process Automation

Semiconductor production and facility workflows

Conductor orchestrates automated workflows for semiconductor manufacturing, including real-time monitoring, alerts and production-line control. Related facility applications coordinate multi-step operations and out-of-control or out-of-specification alerts, connecting the services and information needed to manage fab operations.

Orders and Fulfillment

Logistics workflow modernization

A Conductor-based microservice platform supports the modernization of legacy logistics into event-driven workflows, connecting services involved in the semiconductor supply chain.

Infrastructure Lifecycle

Facility infrastructure provisioning

TSMC engineers built Conductor workflows for facility infrastructure provisioning, connecting GitLab pipelines and automation scripts to set up virtual machines, databases, containers, DNS and mail services.
