Media asset reporting and triage

Device activation

Video retranscoding

Conductor coordinates video retranscoding within Comcast's Unified VOD Ingest platform. Ruby and Go workers connect media-processing stages so that content can move through the required conversion work.

Software Delivery

Workflow platform delivery

Comcast built staging and production environments for Conductor, with delivery pipelines that make application and microservice deployments repeatable. The setup gives engineering teams an environment to test changes before releasing them to production.
