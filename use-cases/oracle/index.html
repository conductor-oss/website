AI agents and business workflows

Retail loan origination

Trade finance workflows

Collections batch processing

Oracle Banking Collections uses Conductor to sequence beginning-of-day, middle-of-day and end-of-day batch jobs. Workflows pass branch and run-date information between services as collections processing progresses.

Corporate credit lifecycle

Conductor coordinates corporate banking processes within Oracle Banking Corporate Finance Process Management. Workflows connect party onboarding, covenant tracking, facility management and collateral services as these capabilities move into a microservices architecture.

Payments and Financial Operations

Oracle Financial Services

Payment and remittance processing

Conductor coordinates multi-step payment and remittance processing, including retries and recovery when dependent services fail. The workflows connect banking service calls into an execution sequence that can be tracked and resumed.

Banking product bundles

Conductor coordinates microservices used to assemble banking product bundles. Workflow execution connects the individual product and service operations required to carry a bundled offering through the application.

Banking workflow routing and tracking

Conductor supports workflow routing, assignment, dependency management and service-level tracking in banking applications. Polling and execution monitoring connect asynchronous service work with the operational view needed to follow each process.
