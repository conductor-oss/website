Document and Content Processing

Studio media processing

Netflix uses its internal Conductor platform to coordinate footage ingestion, validation and transcoding, connecting dailies, visual-effects pulls and conform pulls with studio production workflows.

Marketing and Sales Operations

Advertising creative ingestion

Conductor coordinates advertising-creative ingestion, sequencing processing for each creative and managing branching workflows across the services involved.

Risk and Compliance

Advertiser certificate management

Conductor coordinates advertiser-certificate workflows, connecting integrations with downstream actions when certificates expire.

Data Integration

Data clean room lifecycle

Conductor manages the lifecycle of data clean rooms, coordinating provisioning, partner participation, activation and archival for joint analysis.

Software Delivery

Game distribution

Conductor coordinates partner game builds from ingestion and validation through app-store publishing, including processing associated with iOS privacy manifests.

Document and Content Processing

Promotional artwork production

Conductor coordinates candidate-image selection, processing and review for promotional artwork. The workflow connects video assets with image preparation and title placement, then brings producers into the approval process.

Process Automation

Self service business workflows

Netflix’s finance engineering team built tooling around Conductor so other teams could create and share workflows that connect people and automated services.
