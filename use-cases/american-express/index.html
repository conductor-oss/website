Process Automation

Shared intelligent automation

Conductor underpins an intelligent-automation platform combining business workflows with machine learning, natural-language processing and optical character recognition. Reusable integrations and workflow controls support process, case and work management. A StudioA dashboard makes workflow execution visible for monitoring and review.

Risk and Compliance

KYC document processing

Conductor coordinates document classification, optical character recognition and extraction for KYC and Global Account applications, including key information from identity cards and passports. Related validation, rules and reporting steps connect extracted information with the review work needed to complete a case.

Document and Content Processing

Chargeback invoice processing

Conductor coordinates optical character recognition and processing of merchant invoices used in chargeback disputes. The workflow carries documents through interpretation and the application steps that prepare them for dispute handling.

Document and Content Processing

Automated dispute document review

Conductor coordinates an automated dispute-resolution application that applies OCR and natural-language processing to merchant receipts and correspondence. The workflow connects document interpretation with the steps needed to assess dispute evidence.

Infrastructure Lifecycle

Infrastructure service automation

Conductor forms part of the automation tooling used in global infrastructure and midrange operations. The service environment supports IT service management, infrastructure lifecycle tasks and customer self-service.

Customer Communications

Customer email triage

Conductor coordinates shared-mailbox processing that classifies incoming customer emails and creates service tickets. The workflow connects message interpretation with ticket handling and customer replies.

Customer Communications

Product change notifications

Conductor orchestrates annual product-change communications, connecting customer and product information with email and paper-notice workflows. The implementation supports coordinated delivery of account-related changes to affected customers.

Security Operations

Enterprise data encryption workflows

Conductor connects enterprise automation workflows to an internal cryptographic API for data encryption and decryption, supporting secure storage of enterprise data. The capability forms part of American Express’s Intelligent Automation platform.

Document and Content Processing

Sensitive information redaction

Conductor coordinates automated redaction of sensitive information in documents and images within American Express’s Studio A platform. Workflows connect document-processing services to carry out redaction across submitted material.
